# Kilowott brand reference — v1.0

Compact brand reference for people and AI agents. Live kit: https://brandkit.kilowott.com/project/ · Agent guide: https://brandkit.kilowott.com/AGENTS.md · Source of truth for tokens: `project/styles/tokens.css` (the tables in §2 are generated from it).

> **Tagline:** *Ahead, by design.* · **Long form:** *We build ahead so you can scale faster, deliver better and create impact.* · Middle line (5–8 words): not decided yet; leave a placeholder.
> Kilowott is a strategic partner that turns strategy and execution into measurable outcomes: margins protected, speed gained, delivery you can rely on.

---

## 1. Brand principles

1. **Calm by default.** Generous whitespace, narrow palette. Type does the heavy lifting. Noise is a design failure.
2. **Red earns its place.** Signature red is a spotlight, not a wash. One idea per surface: a stat, a verb, or a CTA.
3. **Editorial over ornamental.** Serif display + clean sans. Italics carry emphasis. No gradients, glows, or filler icons.
4. **Technical and human.** Built with engineers, for enterprise, written like people. Precise, never sterile. Confident, never loud.

### Usage ratio

**60 · 30 · 5 · 5 = paper · ink · warm paper · red.** Paper (`--k-paper`) carries the page, ink (`--k-ink`) holds the type and the occasional dark band, warm paper (`--k-paper-2`) separates sections, red (`--k-red`) is the single spotlight. Never invert it: the brand is paper-led, not ink-led.

### Red rules

1. One red idea per surface: the italic emphasis word in a headline, one stat, the primary CTA, or a thin 1–2px rule. Pick one.
2. Never red body text. Small red text (links, inline code, labels) uses `--link` (= `--accent-ink`, red-ink #8A021B, 10:1 on paper). Brand red is only 4.8:1 on paper and fails on warm paper.
3. Red buttons only for the primary conversion. Everything else is ink or outline.
4. Never tint, gradient, or two-tone red. Never a red background wash or full red section.
5. Never next to another saturated colour.
6. State colours (`--success`, `--warning`, `--info`) are for product UI only, always with an icon and a word. Danger is brand red.

### Alt accents (campaign / sub-brand only)

They **replace** red as the single accent, never sit beside it: Deeper Red `#C8102E` (editorial, print) · Signal Orange `#F05A28` (Workforce; 3.4:1, large type only) · Electric Blue `#1F3CFF` (Intelligence, product) · Ink Mono `#0B0F14`. Default is Kilowott Red `#E4022D`.

## 3. Typography

Exactly three webfonts. System fallbacks only.

- **Display (serif):** Newsreader, roman + italic, weights 400/500, optical size 6–72. Headlines, stats, pull quotes. Never below 20px.
- **Sans (UI and body):** DM Sans, 400/500. Body, UI, buttons, eyebrows. Minimum 12px.
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono, 400/500. Tokens, hex values, folios, table numerals, meta. Never for prose, CTAs, or stat numerals.

Google Fonts (one line):

```html
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com">
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.gstatic.com" crossorigin>
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Newsreader:ital,opsz,wght@0,6..72,400;0,6..72,500;1,6..72,400;1,6..72,500&family=DM+Sans:wght@400;500&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

**Rules**

- Display headlines: Newsreader 400 (not bold), line-height 1.04, tracking −0.02em. One word per headline in `<em>`: italic and red. Only one.
- UI headings (h1–h3 in product surfaces): DM Sans 500.
- Body: DM Sans 16px, line-height 1.55, colour `--fg`, measure 58–64ch.
- Eyebrow: DM Sans 12px caps, tracking 0.18em, 24px leading tick in `currentColor`, max four words. `.eyebrow--accent` makes it red, and then it is the surface's red moment.
- Numbers in columns: `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, right-aligned.
- Headline patterns: italic emphasis · eyebrow + headline · numeric lead (“14× …”).

## 4. Spacing, layout, radii, elevation, motion

- **8pt spacing** (`--s-1` 4px … `--s-10` 128px). Sections are 96px apart (`--s-9`); tight sections 48px (`--s-7`).
- **Layout:** container 1280px, gutter 32px, 12-column grid (4 on mobile), content max 10 columns. Breakpoints 600 / 900 / 1200 / 1440. `grid-2/3/4/6` collapse below 900px.
- **Radii:** 0 · 2 · 4 · 8 (cards, images) · 12 · pill (buttons, chips). Almost nothing else is rounded.
- **Elevation:** no shadow by default; `--shadow-1` hover baseline, `--shadow-2` elevated card or framed image, `--shadow-3` modals only. Dark theme deepens shadows.
- **Motion:** standard 200ms `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`; fast 120ms; slow 320ms. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 8. Imagery

Imagery puts the work in a room. Editorial, not stock.

1. **Real over rendered.** No AI-generated imagery in external work. No glowing “AI” renders or blue grids.
2. **Human gaze.** Real hands, real screens, real sites. No handshakes, no stock smiles, no people lined up against a wall.
3. **Warm, not saturated.** House grade: desaturate slightly, warm slightly. Brand red only when it is already in the frame.
4. **Space for type.** One subject, one idea. Leave room for the headline.

- **Treatments:** house grade (default) · ink/paper duotone for heroes and section covers only · warm mono. Grain 1–3% fine; no filters, vignettes, or gradient overlays.
- **Crops:** 16:9 (hero, case study) · 21:9 (full-bleed hero) · 3:2 · 4:5 (portrait, social) · 1:1 (grids) · 9:16 (stories, reels).
- **Framing:** 8px radius by default, 0 for edge-to-edge heroes, 1px `--rule` border when the image matches the surface tone. On paper with body copy, sit photos in a warm-paper section.
- **Library:** cleared images in `project/assets/photos/`. The Shutterstock originals in `project/uploads/` are licensed stock for Kilowott use only: internal reference library, do not redistribute.

## 9. Voice

### Principles

1. **Outcome-driven.** Measurable results, not activity. Every claim lands on ROI, growth, delivery, or a number you can check.
2. **Strategic and specific.** Partner in the room, not vendor at the door. Name the industry, the tool, the region, the number.
3. **Confident, not loud.** No “may”, “could”, “potentially”. No exclamation marks. The work carries the weight.
4. **Human, not corporate.** Write to a person. Contractions and first person are fine. Senior-partner tone.

### Where we sit on the dial

Formal → **plainspoken** (82%) · Cautious → **direct** (76%) · Activity → **outcomes** (90%) · Vendor → **partner** (86%) · Abstract → **concrete** (86%).

### Service lines (always name the service)

- **Kilowott for Brands** — full operational ownership, measured in ROI
- **Kilowott for Agencies** — white-label and agency partnership
- **Kilowott Intelligence** — the AI offering: smart systems with human oversight
- **Kilowott Workforce** — cross-functional team build-outs

### Vocabulary

- **Use:** partner · deliver / ship · measurable · outcome / ROI · ownership · team / specialists · build · help / support · use
- **Avoid:** leverage · empower · unlock · synergy · best-in-class · cutting-edge · at scale · seamless · end-to-end · tailored solutions · excited to announce · revolutionary / game-changing · transformative · world-class

When in doubt, the left column wins.

### Mechanics (house style)

- **Punctuation:** Oxford comma · em dash unspaced (`word—word`) · semicolons almost never · **no exclamation marks** · curly quotes and apostrophes always · sentence case for titles and buttons.
- **Numbers:** spell out one to nine, numerals for 10 and up · `6%` not “6 percent” · `$4.2M` not `$4,200,000` · en dash for ranges `6–9` · every stat cites a source and a time window.
- **Names:** Kilowott is one word · product names in title case, no “The” · spell out acronyms on first use · lowercase job titles in running text.

### Length ladder

| Length | Format |
|---|---|
| 4–9 words | Website hero line (three-beat taglines by default) |
| 10–14 words | Deck headline (one sentence per slide, argument first) |
| 25–40 words | Subhead / lede |
| under 15 words | Product UI string |
| 60–120 words | Social / LinkedIn post |
| 250–400 words | Service blurb |
| 600–900 words | Case study (situation → decision → result) |
| 1,200–1,800 words | POV / essay (bylined) |

### Register by context

| Context | Tone | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Sales deck | Confident, partner register | 10–14 words per headline |
| Website hero | Sharp, thesis first | 4–9 words |
| Case study | Narrative, ROI-specific | 600–900 words |
| Product UI | Instructive, forgiving | under 15 words per string |
| Social / LinkedIn | Partnered, proof-led | 60–120 words |
| Internal / Slack | Casual, precise | 2–4 lines |

### The same idea, twice

- **Don’t:** “Leveraging cutting-edge AI solutions to empower enterprise transformation at scale.”
  **Do:** “Kilowott Intelligence pairs smart systems with human oversight, so the workflow scales and the outcome stays accountable.”
- **Don’t:** “We drove significant growth through targeted marketing.”
  **Do:** “Organic traffic up 3.5× for Nordhavn Coffee in six months.” *(fictional sample figure)*
- **Don’t:** “We are excited to announce a strategic partnership!”
  **Do:** “A new engagement, starting today—Kilowott Workforce is standing up a cross-functional team with a Nordic logistics partner. First release in Q3.”
